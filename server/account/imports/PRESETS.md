# Import preset reference

Reviewed on November 15, 2026. Fixtures contain invented `example.com` contacts. No platform exports or customer data were copied.

| Platform | Official documentation | Mapping evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| beehiiv | [Export glossary](https://www.beehiiv.com/newsletter-glossary/export), [help center](https://www.beehiiv.com/support) | The glossary describes CSV exports and audience ownership. The subscriber-export help article returned 403 or an empty page, including its Markdown endpoint. Exact current export headings remain unverified. The preset recognizes `subscription_tier`, referral and UTM headings, and maps `email`, `status`, and `created_at`. |
| Substack | [Export your email list](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/6314498343700-How-do-I-export-my-email-list-on-Substack), [subscriber dashboard](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058529871-How-do-I-use-the-subscriber-dashboard-on-Substack) | Retrieved through the public [help-center search API](https://support.substack.com/api/v2/help_center/articles/search.json?query=export&per_page=100), because article HTML returned 403. Export columns depend on the dashboard view. Signup date and subscription type are documented; full names are unavailable. A free or paid plan is never treated as consent or subscription status. |
| Mailchimp | [View and export contacts](https://mailchimp.com/help/view-export-contacts/) | The ZIP contains separate subscribed, unsubscribed, non-subscribed and cleaned CSVs. These files may lack a status column. Choose the file's status explicitly. `Email Address`, `First Name`, `Last Name`, `OPTIN_TIME`, and `LEID` are recognized headings. |
| Kit | [Export subscribers](https://help.kit.com/en/articles/2502489-how-to-export-subscribers-in-kit) | Documents first name, email address, creation time, status, tags, location, attribution and custom fields. Export selection can be restricted to confirmed subscribers. `confirmed` maps to subscribed only when consent is asserted. |
| MailerLite | [Export subscribers](https://www.mailerlite.com/help/how-to-export-subscribers) | Documents selectable fields, groups and active, unconfirmed, unsubscribed or bounced export selections. `Date created` and standard email, name and status headings are mapped. |
| Buttondown | [Subscriber data exports](https://docs.buttondown.com/data-exports-subscriber) | Lists `email`, `subscriber_type`, `creation_date`, `subscription_date`, `unsubscription_date`, `tags`, and metadata. A populated unsubscribe date overrides an active type. Semicolon-separated tags are supported. |
| Ghost | [Import members](https://ghost.org/help/import-members/) | Documents `email`, `name`, `subscribed_to_emails`, `complimentary_plan`, and `labels`. Boolean email consent maps to active or unsubscribed. `created_at` is accepted as the signup date. |
| EmailOctopus | [Export contacts from a list](https://help.emailoctopus.com/article/116-exporting-contacts-from-a-list) | Documents configurable export fields and a separate unsubscribed export. The preset recognizes `Added on` and maps email, first name, last name and status headings. Choose an explicit default status when absent. |
| listmonk | [Subscriber API](https://listmonk.app/docs/apis/subscribers/), [import API](https://listmonk.app/docs/apis/import/) | Documents subscriber state and per-list confirmed, unconfirmed and unsubscribed membership. The preset recognizes `attribs` or UUID/list headings, maps `enabled` to active and `blocklisted` to unsubscribed, and accepts `created_at`. List-specific exports should map their subscription status column. |

Headers are matched without case, spaces, underscores or punctuation. Ambiguous exports remain generic, and the mapping is editable. These sources do not all publish literal CSV schemas. Fixtures exercise representative layouts rather than claiming every platform version has identical headings.

Unknown status values are skipped. Missing status defaults to unconfirmed. Asserted import consent never uses `confirmed`, which is reserved for a contact's own opt-in and can remove newsletter suppressions. Suppressed and deleted contacts remain excluded even when the operator asserts consent.

Signup dates accept Unix seconds, Unix milliseconds, ISO dates, and ISO timestamps with a space or `T`, fractional seconds and optional numeric offset. Timestamps without an offset use UTC. Invalid or future signup dates are reported instead of replaced with today's date.
